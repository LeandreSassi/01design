About me

I received my Master's degree in Architecture at TU Delft in 2018. This sparked my fascination in the use and development of computational tools for architects and designers.

While I have a huge passion for teaching, I also aim to join the three worlds of architecture, computers and education.

I am currently teaching computer science to master 2 students at the faculty of architecture in Delft which provides me the opportunity to sharpen my teaching skills while keeping up to date to the newest technologies regarding my field of interests.

My ambition is to apply this knowledge within the field of architecture.
