Exploring the latent space of a trained facade generator (styleGAN2-ADA-PyTorch)

In this project, I aim at exploring the ways we can have any sense of control of the output of a trained generative AI model.

The model has been trained using the styleGAN2-ADA-PyTorch model and the Flickr API to collect the data.

The PDillis repo styleGAN3-fun has been used in order to generate the interpolation.
